Domain Overview

📖 General Description

Information Assurance (IA) is the systematic practice of protecting information and information systems against unauthorized access, modification, destruction, and disruption.

In today's interconnected digital ecosystems, organizations rely heavily on databases, networks, cloud microservices, and web portals to conduct critical transactions. These systems require rigorous defense-in-depth across people, technology, and organizational policy.

💡
Beyond Simple Passwords
IA is not merely installing an antivirus or setting a PIN. It governs physical security, operational governance, continuous monitoring, and disaster recovery.
Competency Checklist

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Define Information Assurance and explain its strategic role in modern enterprises.
  • Differentiate Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability with real-world SIS scenarios.
  • Identify common cybersecurity threats (malware, phishing, insider threats, DDoS).
  • Conduct Threat Modeling using the STRIDE framework.
  • Distinguish between Threat, Vulnerability, and Risk.
  • Calculate risk scores (\( \text{Risk} = \text{Likelihood} \times \text{Impact} \)) and populate risk matrices.
  • Navigate voluntary cybersecurity standards: NIST CSF 2.0, NIST RMF, ISO/IEC 27001, and OCTAVE.
